# Release provenance

The distributed method prose, authoring/evidence guides and HTML/CSS/JavaScript editor were newly authored for this MIT release from the generic functional contract: a local talk, evidence note, editing, navigation, notes, overview, independent HTML export, phone/print and no-JavaScript reading. No original template implementation was copied into the replacement. This is a provenance statement, not a formal clean-room or legal certification.

Earlier private prototypes used a different adapted implementation. That implementation, its spark artwork and font assets are not included in this public tree. Its original source files, hashes, browser outcomes and failures remain in the private review archive; they are not counted as tests of the new editor. The fictional example HTML is regenerated from the new implementation.

No speaker/client manuscript, private biography, session history, credential or private source collection is bundled. The release uses system font fallbacks and original text/CSS/JavaScript only. No external engine, media service, renderer or installer is embedded.

The package is MIT licensed under the owner's authorization. A caller's evidence, quotations and draft content retain their separate permissions and audience restrictions. Completing or downloading a talk does not grant permission to publish those materials.

Fresh software checks are recorded against the exact replacement artifact hashes and release commit. They establish the tested behavior only, not comparative writing quality, audience comprehension or independent human usefulness.
